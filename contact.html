want to use your gifts?

The vast majority of this summer will be student or young adult led because we are not too young to make an impact and God is honored when we use our gifts to serve him. There will be many opportunities to use your gifts to be a part of this. Listed below are just a few of these opportunities.

  • Worship
  • Speaking/Sharing your testimony
  • Leading prayer
  • Teach others about foreign nations and lead prayer for them
  • Creating graphics for events
  • Creating promo videos for events
  • Setting up spaces for events
  • Developing leadership content
  • Leading evangelism days
  • Planning service opportunities
  • Planning social events and hangouts
  • Planning events to impact the next generation
  • Leading or co-leading a small group

If you are interested in helping lead in any of these areas or have new ideas that are not listed here please let me know! There is no pressure, but God can do so much through a group of people using their gifts together to make an impact. The goal of this summer is not to be led just by one person, but to let God use all of us together and watch what he does.
