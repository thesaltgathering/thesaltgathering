Want to use your gifts?

The vast majority of this summer will be student or young adult. This summer is not about 1 or 2 people, it is about collectively using our gifts, serving, and watching God work.

Please feel free to send us an email for any of the following reasons. We'd love to hear from you.

  • you have an idea for an event
  • you have some other idea you want to share
  • you want to get involved in a specific way
  • you're open to being involved but don't know how
  • you have a prayer request
  • you would like to see something changed
  • you just want to say hi
Email Us!

God has always used ordinary people who feel unqualified. If you feel a desire to make an impact don't let fear stop you.

We hope that at least one of these opportunities will add value to your life and faith. There is no requirement to participate in everything, feel free to get connected and join for whichever events work for you.
