the salt gathering

serving and living together

mission

Summers are a great opportunity. We want to make the most of this summer and grow closer to God, form new friendships, develop deeper community, and have a ton of fun.

We have several ideas of how to make this summer amazing but are always open to more as well!

large group

Every Monday during summer we will have a night of worship, content from the Word, and prayer for each other and for the nations. We will often end the night with something fun and hang out.

service

Throughout the summer there will be opportunities to go into the community and show people who are hurting Christ’s love. There may also be opportunities to evangelize, as well as invest in the younger generation in our communities. We will be looking for ways to impact the world around us together.

fun

There will also be nights to have fun and enjoy fellowship together. Lake days, sports tournaments, ice cream runs, etc. Some of these will be planned ahead of time and some may be spontaneous.

small groups

There will also be an option to join a small group of 3-5 other people for the summer. Your small group can decide what time of the week you want to meet, but when you meet you will check in with each other and share how life is going spiritually and otherwise, as well as study scripture and pray together.

There will also be other fun things like small group hangouts. Small groups are a chance to get closer with other believers and encourage each other over the summer.

leadership

Jesus was always developing leadership in his disciples so that they could carry on the work after He left them. Paul also encourages Timothy to teach others so that they can teach others. Discipleship is everywhere in the Bible. Throughout the summer there will be days dedicated to developing leadership and discipleship.

how will this work?

There will be GroupMe that will be the main form of communication between the whole group throughout the summer. There is where you will find info about large group events, service days, hangouts, etc. It will also be the method of communication whenever spontaneous hangouts happen.

There will be a form sent out with the option to sign up for small groups before the summer starts and then small groups will be established. Small groups are not required, but are a great opportunity to grow spiritually and form close friendships over the summer.

want to use your gifts?

The vast majority of this summer will be student or young adult led because we are not too young to make an impact and God is honored when we use our gifts to serve him. There will be many opportunities to use your gifts to be a part of this. Listed below are just a few of these opportunities.

  • Worship
  • Speaking/Sharing your testimony
  • Leading prayer
  • Teach others about foreign nations and lead prayer for them
  • Creating graphics for events
  • Creating promo videos for events
  • Setting up spaces for events
  • Developing leadership content
  • Leading evangelism days
  • Planning service opportunities
  • Planning social events and hangouts
  • Planning events to impact the next generation
  • Leading or co-leading a small group

If you are interested in helping lead in any of these areas or have new ideas that are not listed here please let me know! There is no pressure, but God can do so much through a group of people using their gifts together to make an impact. The goal of this summer is not to be led just by one person, but to let God use all of us together and watch what he does.
