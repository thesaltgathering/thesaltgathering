mission

Summers are a great opportunity. We want to make the most of this summer and grow closer to God, form new friendships, develop deeper community, and have a ton of fun.

Below are some details on what that will include. Join the GroupMe to stay updated on everything that goes on throughout the summer and stay connected to each other. Sometimes events will happen spontaneously and only be shared through the GroupMe.

salt nights

Every Monday during summer we will have a night of worship, content from the Word, and prayer for each other and for the nations. We will often end the night with something fun and hang out.

small groups

There will also be an option to join a small group of 3-5 other people for the summer. Your small group can decide what time of the week you want to meet, but when you meet you will check in with each other and share how life is going spiritually and otherwise, as well as study scripture and pray together.

There will also be other fun things like small group hangouts. Small groups are a chance to get closer with other believers and encourage each other over the summer.

fun

There will also be nights to have fun and enjoy fellowship together. Lake days, sports tournaments, ice cream runs, etc. Some of these will be planned ahead of time and some may be spontaneous.

service

Throughout the summer there will be opportunities to go into the community and show people who are hurting Christ’s love. There may also be opportunities to evangelize, as well as invest in the younger generation in our communities. We will be looking for ways to impact the world around us together.

leadership

Jesus was always developing leadership in his disciples so that they could carry on the work after He left them. Paul also encourages Timothy to teach others so that they can teach others. Discipleship is everywhere in the Bible. Throughout the summer there will be days dedicated to developing leadership and discipleship.
